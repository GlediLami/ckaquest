# Drill: Custom Columns

## Pattern
`-o custom-columns=NAME:.path,...` and `--sort-by=.path`. The path starts at the object, not at `.items`.

## Exam angle
- Header names are what the task says, in capitals.
- `--sort-by` works with any `-o` format.
